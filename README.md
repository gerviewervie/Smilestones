# Smiles Stone

*To remember and celebrate lives.*

Smiles Stone turns your life story into a lasting legacy, with keepsakes and messages that reach your loved ones at the moments that matter most.

## What this is

This is the first prototype of the Smiles Stone app. People answer guided questions, chapter by chapter, and the app turns their answers into a readable life-story book.

## What it does now

- Seven chapters of guided questions, from "Where I Come From" to "What I've Learned"
- Saves answers automatically in the person's own browser
- "Read my book" view with a cover and chapters
- Copy the whole book to paste into Word or Google Docs

## Not built yet

- Accounts, so stories are saved online and work across devices
- Photos for each chapter
- Voice recording
- Payments and printed books
